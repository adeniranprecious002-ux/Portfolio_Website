01 — About

Background & Skills

I'm an Electrical and Electronics Engineering graduate with deep interest in Embedded Systems, Internet of Things, and intelligent automation. I enjoy building systems that combine hardware and software to solve real-world problems.

My philosophy is simple: hardware first, systems next, intelligence last. Every layer must be solid before adding the next. This means reliable firmware before connectivity, and clean data pipelines before AI.

I'm passionate about solving problems creatively and embedding intelligent software into hardware systems that actually work in the real world.
